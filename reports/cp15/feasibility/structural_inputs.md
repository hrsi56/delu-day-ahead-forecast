# CP-15 structural-input feasibility sheet

**Read-only research; development_post_selection; checked 16 September 2026 (Asia/Jerusalem), 15 September UTC.** No new input entered any model. No fuel layer, fundamental model, purchase, subscription, or license change was performed. Existing `DATA-LICENSE.md` controls the committed snapshot and its derivatives. This sheet identifies future experiments and unresolved acquisition conditions, not a finding that those experiments are ready.

## Decision

The current evidence supports investigating **historical load vintages and pre-coupling cross-border capacity first**. It does not establish a complete, freely redistributable, origin-correct 2019-onward fuel/EUA/outage/weather stack. Fuel prices, generation capacity, and outage availability are different quantities: gas-fired generation volumes cannot substitute for gas prices, and current installed capacity cannot substitute for historically available capacity.

**Verified** below means the current primary-source documentation was retrieved. **Gap** means this session did not verify the required data property. A documentation deadline is not proof that an individual historical value arrived by that deadline. Actual source bytes, retrieval timestamps and SHA-256 digests are indexed in `source_retrieval_manifest.json`; cached documents remain in ignored `data/cp15-probe-cache/sources/`.

## Cross-border publication check: intraday distinction

The [JAO Core intraday handbook v1.6](https://www.jao.eu/sites/default/files/2025-10/Core_IDCC_PublicationTool_Handbook_v1.6.pdf), §5.1, gives initial leftover ATC/NTC publication before 14:30 D-1. These are outputs **after** day-ahead allocation. They fail the 11:00 UTC origin test whether 14:30 means CET, CEST or UTC. Similar-looking “capacity” fields therefore cannot be pooled without their calculation stage. This check validates documented timing, not historical arrival of every individual record.

## Licensing finding that affects every EEX alternative

[EEX DataSource General Conditions v8](https://www.eex.com/fileadmin/EEX/Downloads/Market_Data/EEX_Group_DataSource/General_Terms_of_Contract/WIP_Datasource_GC-v8_FINAL.pdf), effective November 2025, distinguishes usage rights in §§3.1–3.2 and explicitly addresses AI use in §3.2.3. A suitable licensed use is required; displaying a number publicly is not evidence that a purchaser may redistribute a historical dataset in this repository. No license was bought or changed. Exact product-specific terms and any applicable exception remain unresolved here. The existing `DATA-LICENSE.md` is neither modified nor extended to these proposed sources.

The ENTSO-E October 2023 reuse list is scoped by **data item and primary owner**, with explicit country/interconnector exclusions. Its load and cross-zonal/transmission entries are useful, but it is not a license for everything visible on the platform. This distinction concerns new-source feasibility; it does not retroactively reinterpret the inherited SMARD snapshot contract.

## Minimum evidence required before a later experiment

The following are proposed admission checks, not new CP-15 governance:

1. Define one product, unit, territory, granularity and historical start at or after **2019-01-01**. Separate technical availability from commercial redistribution rights. A stable permalink plus dated terms is needed for each input.
2. Store raw release/version identity with **issued_at**, **available_at**, **valid_from/to**, retrieval time, timezone, status, provider and checksum. Apply the unchanged origin in UTC before any joins or imputations.
3. Quantify missingness by input, year, date/hour, outage threshold, country and source version. Preserve DST’s actual 23/24/25 hours and quarter-hour completeness. Distinguish absent values from genuine zeros, market holidays and delayed releases.
4. Freeze a forward-fill age limit or training-only imputation rule in a future authorized protocol; report its sensitivity. No silent patch with revised observations, reanalysis or a later source regime.
5. Demonstrate complete admissible coverage and redistribution rights before estimating a fuel or merit-order model. This session supplies neither a benchmark ranking nor evidence that structural inputs improve 2022 predictions.

**Interview-answer trigger for the Orchestrator:** we distinguished a downloadable historical series from data that was actually available at forecast time; publication-stage and revision checks prevented post-auction capacity and revised outages from masquerading as causal inputs.
