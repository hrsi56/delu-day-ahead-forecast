## Lessons

- **A result far from the prior is a reason to widen the controls before the review,** not after.
- **A test that pins living files is a landing hazard.** Prove "unchanged" for one commit, and
  keep CI's permanent tests to what must never change.
- **Report intervals at the decision level.** The rule decided at 97.5%, and the report showed
  ratios at 95% only.
- **A nested Critic dies with its Lead.** Commit before opening the review, and keep each step
  resumable from the branch and the ledgers.
