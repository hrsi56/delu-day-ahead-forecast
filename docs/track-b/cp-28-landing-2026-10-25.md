These are the results on the 2,068 paired eligible days, from `reports/cp28/owner-summary.json`.

|---|---:|---:|
| B0 | 34.553 | 21.142 |

- **The gates.** All seven arms rejected their compound null under Holm at α = 0.05, in the
  primary analysis and in the 7-day, 28-day and unstratified variants. No arm was inconclusive.
  A1's compound p-values were 0.0042, 0.0069, 0.0022 and 0.0053.
- **The selection.** A1 has the lowest WIS, and no arm lies within 0.5% of it, so it is selected. It
  is better than the control in every year and season cell.
- **Startup and fallbacks:**
  - 364 learning dates were reached on 2021-03-06, so the learned arms ran at v5's weights on 67
    scored dates;
  - 728 were reached on 2022-03-12, so A4 equals A3 on 370 scored dates;
  - each arm has 18 fallback dates with a missing member (2022-12-04 to 2023-06-20). There was no
    solver failure.
- **The GFS note** (descriptive, before any arm ran):
  - GFS +6 h worsens nominal v5 by +14.0% MAE and +15.5% WIS;
  - on the affected dates, v2 is within about 1% of the delayed-weather route;
  - no availability-switching experiment is recommended on present evidence.
