# CP-21 publication plan — both outcomes (PRES-3)

**Orchestrator planning document, 2026-09-29. Not a brief and not an authority.** It plans the
block that follows CP-21's landing, so that CP-21's result reaches the public site in either
outcome, as the Owner requires. The PRES-3 brief is issued only after CP-21's terminal return
and the Owner's LAND. That brief names its own ceilings and every external action the Owner
authorizes. The Owner ratified this routing as decision D5 on 2026-09-29; see the
[v21-r5 → v21-r6 amendment record](capstone_v21-r5-to-v21-r6-amendments.md).

## 1. Decision (D5): a separate publication block

Run publication as its own block (PRES-3) immediately after the Owner lands CP-21, not as a
phase inside CP-21.

- **Landing identities.** Runbook §2 step 1 dates adoption at landing, with the landing record
  as its source. Steps 2–3 need the evidence tag and its freeze date. None of these exists
  inside the checkpoint.
- **Different work per outcome.** An adopted v4 needs a chapter, the A3 transition, a new
  headline and the Owner's encoding. A not-adopted result needs a branch card. CP-21's packet
  drafts only the outcome its rule yields.
- **Different reviews.** The research Integration Critic judges the experiment. Publication
  needs an editorial review, a fresh cold reader and an independent publication check
  (PUBLISH_RULES §10). Separating them keeps each review focused.
- **Named authority.** The MLflow upload, the push, the Pages deployment and the Space upload
  each need the Owner's explicit instruction naming the action (`AGENTS.md`).
- **Precedent.** CP-20 → PRES-1/PRES-2 ran this way.

## 2. Entry conditions

1. CP-21 returned PASS, with a fresh Integration PASS and its publication packet and draft
   export at the evidence tip. CP-21 registers no public entries, because statuses are dated at
   landing.
2. The Owner squash-landed it and tagged `land/cp-21` and `evidence/cp-21`, and its branch was
   reclaimed.
   - An Engineering-PASS CP-21 is proposed for LAND in either research outcome, because the page
     derives every number from committed rows on `main`.
3. An INCOMPLETE or BLOCKED CP-21 has no result to publish, so no PRES-3 follows. The Owner
   re-briefs or closes it.

## 7. Sequence and authority (PUBLISH_RULES §11; runbook §1)

1. Build from the packet.
2. Editorial review.
3. Fresh cold reader: six questions, rendered screens only.
4. Independent check at the candidate SHA.
5. MLflow upload, then verification, route checks and index, on the Owner's named instruction.
6. `build_pages.py --final`.
7. Focused recheck on the final SHA.
8. Landing, push, Pages and Space deployment, each on the Owner's named instruction.
9. Public post-deployment checks (A6), keeping first failures and retries.
10. Packet §8 receipt and a closure record.

No placeholder or non-final build reaches `main`. Pre-push guards fail closed, and the secret
guard is never bypassed.
