## Scientific result

These are development evidence after selection, on the five folds that CP-15, CP-20 and CP-21
also used. Intervals are 95%, from the shared 2,000-replicate bootstrap.

**`cp22-replacement`: no replacement.**

| Policy | Construction | Equal-fold ΔS against v4 (MAE / WIS) | Fold-4 MAE difference (EUR/MWh) |
|---|---|---|---|
| R | One pooled normalized LightGBM averaged over four capacities | +0.0011 / +0.0010 | +0.31 [0.15, 0.42] |
| M | The split removed | −0.0013 / −0.0008 | +0.20 [0.05, 0.33] |

- **Conditions 1–3 met.** Both policies were non-inferior to v4: all four overall intervals span
  zero. Both met all six §8 diagnostics and issued all 10,747 keys.
- **Condition 4 failed** for both, as their first unmet condition. Fold 4 (2025-05-01..07-29) is
  decisively worse than v4 in MAE and in WIS. No other fold is.

**`cp22-dynamic-layer` and `cp22-fast-component`:** not applicable, because there is no winner W.
The W+ACI, W+DL and W+DLF arms were not run.

**Descriptive findings:**

- **The ladder.** No step shows a demonstrated preference:

  | Step | ΔS_MAE | ΔS_WIS |
  |---|---|---|
  | v4 → M (the split removed) | −0.0013 | −0.0008 |
  | M → A-PN-sel (the raw half dropped) | +0.0015 | +0.0005 |
  | A-PN-sel → R (averaging instead of daily selection) | +0.0010 | +0.0012 |

  v4's gain over v3 comes from adding the LightGBM member.
- **The dynamic interval layer.**
  - It reacted to the 2022 peak in 4 days rather than 12.
  - It widened the 95% intervals by about 15%.
  - It worsened S_WIS by 4.45% [3.12, 6.07] on v4 and by 4.34% [3.04, 5.88] on v3.
- **Capacity selection** is mostly noise: PN's flip rate is 0.54, with a median margin of 1.1%.
  LEAR's penalty selection is stable, with a flip rate of about 0.10.
- **The member-weight curve** is an oracle and selects nothing. Its central optimum lies around
  0.45–0.55, against the fixed 1/3.

**Not established:**

- any result on new data;
- any release, designation or Live status.
