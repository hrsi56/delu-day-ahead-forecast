## 1. The day-ahead load forecast (tier 3): a recorded exception, plus a no-load check

**The finding.** The forecast's deadline is D−1 10:00 local, one hour before the issue time.
Regulation 543/2013 Art. 6(2)(b) and SMARD require updates on significant (≥10%) change. The
snapshot holds SMARD's values as pulled on 2026-09-08, with no vintages. §24.5 therefore treats the
input as tier 3. No tier-1 or tier-2 substitute exists in the preserved data.

**The ruling (Owner, 2026-10-12): a recorded exception.**

- **The main evaluation keeps the load forecast.** CP-26 runs B0, v1-R and v2–v5 with their load
  inputs as landed.
- **The control stays v5,** as landed (§24.5).
- **The revision risk is disclosed** wherever CP-26's or CP-28's results are reported.
- **Why.** In live operation the forecast is published before the issue time, so it is a genuinely
  available input. The doubt concerns only whether the stored history equals the first issue.

**The no-load check: one descriptive variant, "v5 (no load forecast)".** It bounds how much any
revision in the stored history could flatter the results. Its construction, as declared method
changes applied by CP-26's harness and no others:

- **LEAR (A1_w, B2_w, and A1 and B2 where used):** the load columns are removed, including the D,
  D−1 and D−7 load lags.
- **The three-block LightGBM (L-N, L-R):** the load features are removed.
- **DDNN-2:** the always-included `load_d0` and the optional D−1 and D−7 load groups are removed,
  with the search space adjusted to match. Its 14 searches are rerun under §24.7's schedule.
- **The blend:** the weights (2/3, 1/6, 1/6), with the H layer re-estimated on the variant's own
  errors.
- **The parity check (§24.5):** with the removed inputs restored, the variant's code reproduces the
  as-landed parity vectors.
- **Its status:** descriptive only. It is never the control, a CP-28 member or arm, or a candidate
  for the freeze. CP-26 reports v5 against the variant: the overall paired differences, and by year
  and season.

**The ceiling.** The variant costs about 70–85 machine-hours, so CP-26's ceiling rises from 400 to
**485 machine-hours** (§24.5). The timing pilot's 80% stop line moves with it.

## 2. The target price: tier 2

**The finding.**

- CACM Art. 47(5) makes matched orders firm, and Art. 39(2)(a) gives one clearing price per zone and
  market time unit.
- Under Art. 48, TSOs and NEMOs verify the results before delivery.
- Regulation 543/2013 Art. 12 sets publication within one hour of the gate, with no update wording.
- **The caveat:** SMARD writes that a later update of published prices "ist möglich". The snapshot
  matches ENTSO-E A44 on all 120 sampled hours.

**The ruling (Owner, 2026-10-12):** tier 2, with the SMARD caveat disclosed.
